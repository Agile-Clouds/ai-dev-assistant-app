# Changes

## 0.15.0 — 2026-10-25

- Allowing another browser or phone now means typing the 8 digits the new device shows.
- A browser allowed less than a day ago cannot allow others yet, and every new browser is announced.
- A check that never started because your PC was busy waits and tries again, instead of counting as a failure.
- A task folder held after an unconfirmed Windows start frees itself without restarting your PC.
- Milestone proofs work on large projects.
- Publishing is faster on Windows.
- Move online follows the sign-up setting.
- New online terms: email accounts and the locked online dashboard.
- The final audit also tightened checks for secrets and for changes merged together. It improved how Windows keeps tasks apart, and fixed chat and planning in the locked online dashboard.

## 0.14.24 — 2026-10-23

- Your PC keeps each picture and attachment in its own file, so its records stay small and fast.
- A project server's browser sign-in renews by itself.
- A task that needs a sign-in says so and offers **Sign in**.

## 0.14.23 — 2026-10-23

- Pictures, chat and attachments stay on your PC after it goes back from online.
- Every setting says what it does and its default.
- Move online sends your projects first.
- The words about what your PC sends and what the team can see are clearer.
- AIs keep temporary files in their own session folder.
- **New task** and **Plan** stay in a project's slim header.
- A chosen publish host stays chosen.
- A task's next question no longer shows an old result.

## 0.14.22 — 2026-10-23

- A task no longer stops to ask when an AI only reads where npm keeps its programs.
- Installing a program for the whole PC still needs your decision.

## 0.14.21 — 2026-10-23

- A task whose browser has no page yet starts without it and says why.
- A tool that cannot start offers **Start without it**.
- Publish, preview and Roll back each write one line in your PC's log when they start and end.

## 0.14.20 — 2026-10-23

- Presses on the online dashboard no longer fail because the PC locked its key between two requests.
- Move online opens the dashboard about twice as fast over a real internet connection.
- A project's MCP servers can use a web address, a key or a browser sign-in.
- A long-open dashboard reopens its browser storage by itself.

## 0.14.19 — 2026-10-23

- A new project on Autopilot keeps planning after its first plan.
- Stopping a cloud task keeps its link and says that the cloud keeps running, with **Continue** or **Bring it back to this PC**.
- The browser locks down the Assistant's drawings.
- A project can use its own MCP servers, scripts, folders and programs by path, with clearer states.
- Presses after Move online are not lost or answered twice.
- The Publish card does not pick **My own command** by itself.
- A GitHub Pages address keeps its path.

## 0.14.18 — 2026-10-22

- Move online takes about 1.5 seconds without a restart.
- You can add phones after the move.
- Netlify previews and publishes were confirmed on a real account.
- A stopped preview offers **Try again**.
- The move keeps your email choice.
- **Bring it back to this PC**, **Waits for you** and the Railway token page were added.

## 0.14.17 — 2026-10-22

- **Update now** has one source for its update.
- A milestone is proved again after a fix that changed no files.
- A preview can ask for its missing files in a task.
- Netlify publishing works on an installed PC.

## 0.14.16 — 2026-10-22

- A milestone older than the project's checks says so once.
- A passing test whose file builds its name shows that cause.
- Test names are treated as plain text.

## 0.14.15 — 2026-10-22

- A marked milestone without checks waits for **Accept anyway**.
- A failed planning step offers **Start over**.
- **Looks right** answers at once.
- Planning sits beside finished work.
- Approval, build-size, preview and refused-command words are clearer.
- Going online is faster.

## 0.14.14 — 2026-10-22

- **Change something** and **Something's missing** change the planning meeting itself.
- Adding a feature works after you have answered one of the plan's decisions.
- A refused plan says what differed.

## 0.14.13 — 2026-10-22

- An open locked dashboard uses about 7 MB an hour instead of 17 to 38 MB while a task works.
- A milestone marked done by its reviewed task can publish.
- A planning meeting saved before an update continues on **Retry**.
- Adding a feature keeps your goal, go-ahead list and **Not now** choices.

## 0.14.12 — 2026-10-22

- **Add a feature** keeps the other milestones as they are and asks the AI to write only the new one.
- The AIs know which milestones your PC proves and which a task marks done.
- A stopped task says **Stopped by you**.
- Linux and Mac checks pass again.

## 0.14.11 — 2026-10-21

- Pressing the icon while the dashboard is open brings it forward instead of opening another window.
- A new project planned with **Write the plan** starts its tasks.
- You can choose a task's tools in **New task**, or change them after stopping a task.
- A failed GitHub call says which call failed and why.
- A task says you chose how it is built only when you did.

## 0.14.10 — 2026-10-21

- Going online opens the dashboard in about a second.
- An open dashboard tab uses about one thousandth of the data it used before.
- You can try a milestone online before accepting it, or download it as an app.
- The sign-in page has clear **Forgot your password** and **Ask to join** links.
- A PC taken offline works again at once.
- A short network error no longer stops a moved PC's work.
