# Changes

## 0.15.5

- Your own plan is read the way you wrote it: one line per job from its tables and lists, never cut in the middle of a word, "Done when" only from your own words, and your on-hold items in Not now.
- A review of your plan lets you choose Apply or Ignore for each suggestion first; one button then writes the plan with your choices and says which AI writes it, how long it takes and how it is paid. Suggested lines no longer look like links. A plan written from your own plan keeps your milestones exactly: a suggestion you ignored is never written in, and no milestone is added.
- Saving a plan installs and checks once, not twice, when nothing changed meanwhile, and leaves one copy of your project instead of two.
- In the planning meeting no choice can change while its press is on its way, Stop always works, a waiting card always ends, and the cost line is gone. Areas you answered, now or in an earlier meeting, are green.
- Add project: Look for it runs one search at a time and stops spinning; only real settings are listed as keys; anything that needs you after Add is in Needs you; the Dev server line is green when it is ready.
- Settings, Keys: one list of the keys on your PC, and Add a key works when a key is already saved.
- Alerts are newest first on the online dashboard; First steps no longer shows a lone number; the go-ahead list refuses a change made on an outdated copy.
- Settings, Tools says how to add another tool: ask the project's Assistant.
- Words that are cut end at a whole word with "…", and the added page says which AI read your project.

## 0.15.4 — 2026-10-27

- Every button and setting shows your change the moment you press it, with words that say what it is doing, until
  your PC confirms it; a refusal puts it back with the reason. A press the dashboard did not take says "Not sent yet",
  and one your PC has not received says "Not delivered yet"; pressing again sends it once, never twice.
- The online dashboard sees its own presses again: after 40 presses it lost track of new ones, so settings flashed
  back, the rules list never arrived and Save stayed on "Saving…". A press's effect now shows with its answer.
- The planning meeting: the card you answered folds into one working card at once, with what your PC is doing, the
  time so far and Stop. Steps are no longer cut at a fixed time, and a quiet AI gets one more try by itself. Review it
  shows its findings. A goal written over several lines is read whole. Start over asks first and keeps your meeting.
  Your answers apply when you approve What I understood. Draft a plan now says plainly what it still asks.
- Saving an approved plan shows each step with its time: preparing, installing, each check, sending, saving on main.
- After your plan is published, one card in Needs you lists the tools, servers, skills, AI settings and checks your
  project offers, with Approve, Not now and Approve all; a key a task waits for is approved in Needs you too.
  Milestone 1's card shows every first task the planner proposed.
- When your plan chose a host, a question asks whether to turn Publish on: at the plan's publication, and once more
  at your next accepted milestone. It never guesses the host.
- Settings: the go-ahead list is in Permissions too; help lines no longer overlap; changing the dev server's port
  really starts it on that port, and Clear keeps its start command; results name what changed ("1 hour").
- Codex cloud: OpenAI's refusal is shown in its own words with what to do (for example two-step sign-in), with the
  legacy environments page your PC needs; a failed trial never reads as connected.
- Adding a project with its own plan starts in seconds and says plainly what it read.
- Keys stay hidden from every log, alert and screen, even when two of them overlap.

## 0.15.3 — 2026-10-26

- Adding a project that already has a plan: the planning screen shows your product's goal and one short line per
  milestone, says what it found and where, and recommends **Review it**, **Show me your plan** (tick the files that
  hold your plan) or **Start a new plan**, depending on what it could read.
- A newly added project always gets its planning meeting first, even when you approve it while Add is still reading,
  when the PC restarts during Add, or when the account had the project before; its first plan's publication lets the
  tasks start.
- Add project asks only what you decide at the start: no About or Checks and install sections, **Tasks and
  milestones** instead of Pace, and your PC's AI setup shown for all projects with links to change it in Your PC.
- **What needs your go-ahead first** now holds Autopilot: proposals and merges that touch a kind of change on your list
  wait for your OK; remove a kind to let it run without asking. The field suggests 14 ready-made kinds, each with what
  it covers, and refuses a kind already on the list.
- The planning meeting asks which pages to picture before and after each change, and asks when your choices
  contradict your project's own rules.
- Codex cloud checks your environment's name as you type it.
- The sign-in check names Claude's API-key way when you use it.
